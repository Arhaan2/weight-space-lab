# IFEval browser V1: descriptive review of changed outputs

Authorship: [Arhaan Aggarwal (Arhaan2)](https://github.com/Arhaan2).

The saved outputs show mixed changes in instruction following, formatting, termination, and repetition. Some candidate responses finish a requested structure or stay within a length limit. Other official gains come from repeated highlights, repeated sentences, or punctuation removal without a substantive improvement. Regressions include lost response structure, forbidden words, missing keywords, and shorter responses that miss minimum-length requirements. These observations do not establish a general reduction in repetition or an improvement in substantive answer quality.

This is descriptive inspection, separate from the [completed official comparison](../reports/ifeval-browser-v1-heldout-continuation-combined/README.md). It does not change any grade, add an endpoint, or assign a post-hoc usefulness score. No model or checker was run for this review. The frozen instruction flags identified the pairs to inspect; paired wins and losses were not inferred from the aggregate score difference.

The review joined the saved 128 baseline records with the original candidate's 94 completed records and the separately completed 34-record continuation. It compared corresponding saved strict and loose instruction flags and inspected the changed outputs and constraints. Exactly **19 of 128 pairs** differ in at least one instruction flag: 17 in the original candidate session and two in the continuation. The other 109 pairs have unchanged instruction outcomes, which does not imply identical text. The table covers every changed pair; positions are 1-based positions in the original frozen held-out schedule. “Gain” and “loss” describe the saved official prompt outcome, and “fail tie” means both prompt outcomes fail.

The continuation contributes only the last two rows: both prompt metrics regress at positions 98 and 114, while its other 32 pairs have unchanged instruction flags. The complete held-out strict-prompt totals remain eight improvements, seven regressions, and 113 ties; the loose-prompt totals remain ten improvements, seven regressions, and 111 ties. These are counts from paired saved grades, not a new score or a classification of usefulness.

The sole development change was **development position 17**, a separate schedule from the table above. It required at least 22 highlighted sections. The baseline echoed long plot text with three complete highlighted spans; the candidate repeated one short title, producing 169 complete highlighted spans. Both truncated at 1,020 sampled tokens. The saved strict and loose fail-to-pass grades are preserved. The development selection gain therefore reflects repeated content satisfying a formatting checker, not better substantive answering or reduced repetition.
